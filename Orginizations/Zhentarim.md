Powerful organization that seeks to exert secret control over the North through wealth and influence.

# Members
- [[Halia Thornton]] | Working to slowly bring [[Phandalin]] under her control.

# Joining The Zhentarim
If the party dispatches the [[Redbrands]]' leader, [[Halia Thornton]] converses with the party to determine if they are candidates for membership in the Zhentarim. She's looking for people focused on wealth, power and influence. If any character meets her approval, she privately urges them to join the Zhentarim. She gives the individual the title of "Fang" and a pin that represent the Zhentarim.
