A group of faithful seekers of justice who protect others from depredations of evildoers. The order is always vigilant, ready to smite evil, enfore justice, and enact retribution against any who try to subjugate or harm others.

## Members
- [[Daran Edermath]] | No longer active, keeps an eye on happenings around [[Phandalin]].

# Joining The Order
If the party resolves [[Daran Edermath#Daran's Suspicions|"Redbrands"]] and [[Daran Edermath#Old Owl Trouble|Old Owl Trouble]], [[Daran Edermath]] will invite the players to the order. He speaks with those who exemplify the virtues of the order. If a character agrees, Daran awards the individual the title of Chervall and hands them a pin that represents the order.
