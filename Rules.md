# Homebrew rules
We will be using the 2014 rules.
## 1. Potions
- Potions can be thrown for a distance of **30ft**. shattering on impact affecting any creature within **5ft.**
- When you consume a **healing** potion as an **Action**, you regain the potion's full listed amount. 
- When consumed a **healing** potion using a **Bonus** Action, you regain the listed amount.
```
Potion           | Action | Bonus Action
Healing Potion   | 10     | 2d4 + 2
Greater Healing  | 20     | 4d4 + 4
Superior Healing | 40     | 8d4 + 8
Supreme Healing  | 60     | 10d4 + 20
```
## 2. Critical Hits
- Critical Hits now deal all **dice maximum + dice rolls + modifier damage**. E.g. if your normal critical hit does `1d10 + 1d10(Crit) + 1d4 + 1d4(Crit) + 3 damage` it's now `10(Crit) + 4(Crit) + 1d10 + 1d4 + 3 damage`.
## 3. Attunement
- Attunement to a magical item only takes `1 action` instead of `1 hour / Short Rest`.
- Removing attunement takes `1 minute`.
- There will be no abusing this.
## 4. Flanking
- You can flank an enemy if you and an ally are on **direct opposite sides of a creature**. 
- Flanking grants a `+2` bonus to hit.
## 5. Hit Points
- When gain a **level** take the highest value on your **Hit Die**. That is the amount of maximum **Hit Points** you gain for that level.
- When you roll one or more **Hit Dice** during a short rest, any die result lower than your **Constitution** modifier is treated as equal to your **Constitution** modifier.
## 6. Bonus Action Spells
- Leveled **bonus** action spell can be cast on the same turn as a normal leveled spell.
## 7. Exhaustion
- We will be using the **2024** (5.5e) rules for **exhaustion**.
## 8. Grappling
- We will use the **2024** (5.5e) rule for grappling.
- DC of 8 + STR + prof.
## 9. Ammunition
- We will not be keeping track of basic ammunition.
## 10. Spell Components
- You do not need spell components unless mentioned otherwise. 
- Exceptions are: Revivify, Resurrection, True Resurrection, ... Any spell may be added at any time.



# Character Creation Rules
## 1. Standard Point Array
- We will be using the standard point array for **ability scores**.
- Pick each number once and assign it to a ability score (15-14-13-12-10-8)
## 2. No evil alignment
- No characters with an **evil alignment**, i don't think i am equipped to deal with those. 
## 3. Races
- No **monstrous** races.
- No **flying** races.
## 4. Level 1 feat
- You can pick a feat when you normally would get an ASI.
- You may pick any **feat at level 1**. Feels like it would make low level play more fun.
## 5. Tasha's Cauldron of Everything
- You are encouraged to use the **optional class features** from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything.
## 6. Background
- Create some kind of link to Gundren Rockseeker
- Optionally create a link to Phandalin.
- Have a reason why you would be in Neverwinter at the start of the adventure.
## 7. Avoid AI
- Just avoid using AI for writing character backstory or art. It's just kind of meh.
