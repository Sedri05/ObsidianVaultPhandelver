[[Item]]
**Type:** Pendant
**Rarity:** Artifact
**Attunement:** Yes
**Description:** A large emerald the shape of a heart sits within a wooden frame. When picked up it transforms in to a smaller variant that has a golden chain so it can be worn around your neck.
**Bonus:** +1 Wisdom saving throws.
**Charges:** /
# Abilities
When attuned gain proficiency in nature checks, if you already have proficiency gain expertise instead.
Additionally you can cast Revivify once per day without requiring material components, when you do so take necrotic damage equal to half your remaining Hit Points. This damage can not be reduced in any way.

Alternatively you can draw on all the power within the artifact at once and cast Resurrection, after this the artifact crumbles in to green dust and the wooden frame instantly rots.
## Hidden Feature
If consumed by a dragon that dragon instantly ages up to its next variant. E.g. a young dragon becomes adult, and an adult dragon becomes ancient.
