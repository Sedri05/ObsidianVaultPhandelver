[[Item]]
**Type:** Ring
**Rarity:** Uncommon
**Attunement:** Yes, Any
**Description:** A gold ring, inscribed with a small heart on the inside.
**Bonus:** /
**Charges:** 2d10/30 when found.
# Abilities
This ring can store up to 30 hit points. While wearing this ring, you can use an action to spend one or more hit dice to store in the ring. For each Hit Die spent in this way, you roll the die and add your Constitution modifier to it. Hit Dice spent on this ring's features can't be used to regain hit points during a short rest. You regain spent Hit Dice as normal.
When hit points are stored in the ring, you can cause one of the following effects: 
- You can use a bonus action to remove stored hit points from the ring and regain that number of hit points.  
- You can use an action to remove stored hit points from the ring while touching the ring to a creature. If you do so, the creature regains hit points equal to the amount of hit points you removed from the ring.  
- When you are reduced to 0 hit points and are not killed outright, you can use a reaction to empty the ring of stored hit points and regain hit points equal to that amount.

## Alternate version
This ring can store up to 3 hit dice. While wearing this ring, you can use an action to store on or more Hit Dice inside the ring. These Hit Dice cannot be used to regain hit points during a short rest. You regain spend Hit Dice as normal. At dawn the ring loses 1 Hit Dice stored in it, if it has any.
When Hit Dice are stored in this ring, you can cause one of the following effects:
- You can use an Action to spend 1 Hit Die. Take the maximum possible result on that die and add your Constitution modifier to regain that many Hit Points.
- You can use a Bonus Action to spend 1 Hit Die. Roll that die and add your Constitution modifier to regain that many Hit Points.
- You can use an Action to spend 1 stored Hit Die from the ring while touching the ring to a creature. Roll that die and add your Constitution modifier, the creature you touched regains that many Hit Points.
- When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points and are not killed outright, you can use a Reaction to empty the ring of stored Hit Dice. Roll each die and add your Constitution modifier to regain that many Hit Points. When you use this feature roll a d20, on a result of 1, the ring is destroyed.
