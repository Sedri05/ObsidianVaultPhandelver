[[Item]]
**Type:** Ring
**Rarity:** Rare
**Attunement:** Yes
**Description:** A ring made of a single piece of mystical green glass that's stronger than steel.
**Bonus:** +1 AC
**Charges:** /
# Abilities
You gain a **+1** bonus to [Armor Class](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#ArmorClass) and saving throws while wearing this ring.
You also gain **+4** bonus to saving throws or checks to avoid dropping or being disarmed of any item you held in that hand.
