[[Item]]
**Type:** Helmet
**Rarity:** Uncommon
**Attunement:** Yes, by paladin.
**Description:** A steel helmet with a set of runes that have been expertly worked into the filigree. 
**Bonus:** +1 Constitution saves.
**Charges:** /
# Abilities
When attuned to this item you gain a +1 bonus to your Constitution Saving Throws. 
Additionally when you use divine smite or any of its variants, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier.
