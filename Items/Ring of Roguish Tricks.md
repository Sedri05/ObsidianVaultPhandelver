# Abilities
The ring allows for cunning ways to use your Sneak Attack. When you deal Sneak Attack damage, you can add one of the following Cunning Strike effects. Each effect has a die cost, which is the number of Sneak Attack dice you must forgo to add the effect. You remove the die before rolling, and the effect occurs immediately after the attack's damage is dealt. For example, if you add the Poison effect, remove 1d6 from the Sneak Attack's damage before rolling. The DC of the effect is 8 + Proficiency + Dexterity Modifier.

**Poison (Cost: 1d6).** You add a toxin to your strike, forcing the target to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the Poisoned condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the poisoned target repeats the save, ending the effect on a success. To use this effect, you must have a Poisoner's Kit on your person.

**Trip (Cost: 1d6).** If the target is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or have the Prone condition.

**Withdraw (Cost: 1d6).** Immediately after the attack, you move up to half your speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

**Disarm (Cost: 1d6).** The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or drop its weapon.

**Push (Cost: 1d6).** If the target is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away from you.

**Distract (Cost 1d6).** The next attack roll against the target by an attacker other than you has advantage if the attack is made before the start of your next turn.

**Disrupt (Cost 1d6).** If the target is currently concentrating on a spell, it has disadvantage on the saving throw from this attack.

**Mutilate (Cost 1d6).** The target is unable to regain hit points until the start of your next turn.

**Ricochet (Cost: 2d6).** Immediately after hitting a target with a ranged or thrown weapon attack, you can target a different creature within 10 feet of the original target. Make a single weapon attack roll against the second creature. On a hit, it takes damage equal to half the original damage. 

**Daze (Cost: 2d6).** The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or on its next turn, it can do only one of the following: move or take an action or a Bonus Action.

**Obscure (Cost: 3d6).** The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw, or it has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn.

**Knock Out (Cost: 6d6).** The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or it has the Unconscious condition for 1 minute or until it takes any damage. The Unconscious target repeats the save at the end of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
