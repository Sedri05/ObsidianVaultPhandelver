[[Item]]
**Type:** Longsword
**Rarity:** Rare
**Attunement:** Yes
**Description:** Longsword with a die as a hilt. Talon on the hilt. **DC15 History** check reveals that the sword belonged to the a great knight named [[Aldith Tresendar]], known as the Black Hawk. He died fighting off the bandits throughout the hidden caverns. The sword was lost until [[Ssarnak]] found it.  
**Bonus:** +1d4-1 At dawn.
**Charges:** /
# Abilities
Roll 1d4-1 as a magical bonus. This sword gains that bonus to its attack and damage rolls. You also take a corresponding penalty (0 to −3) to your death saving throws. This magical bonus is rolled each day at dawn.
### Cursed
This weapon is cursed, and becoming attuned to it extends the curse to you. Until the curse is broken with a Remove Curse spell or similar magic, you are unwilling to part with the weapon. Additionally when you roll a natural 1 on any of your attack rolls, you have disadvantage on your next attack role.
