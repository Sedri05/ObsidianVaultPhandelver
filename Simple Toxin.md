[[Item]]
**Type:** Toxin
**Rarity:** Common
**Attunement:** No
**Description:** A small vial filled with green liquid.
**Bonus:** /
**Charges:** 1
# Abilities
Whenever this items is used in the following ways it is consumed.

As a bonus action, you can apply this toxin to a weapon or object. The toxin remains active for 1 minute or until you hit a creature with the coated object, whichever comes first. When you hit a creature with a coated object, the creature becomes poisoned for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, it must make a **DC 10 Constitution saving throw**. On a success, the poisoned condition ends. On a failure, the creature takes **1d4 poison damage**.

Alternatively, you can throw the vial at a point within range, shattering it on impact. Each creature within 5 feet of that point becomes poisoned for 1 minute. At the end of each affected creature's turn, it must make a **DC 10 Constitution saving throw**. On a success, the poisoned condition ends. On a failure, the creature takes **1d4 poison damage**.
