# Natural Hot Springs
```
When you enter this room you hear the sound of running water. This large natural cavern has a small stone step up to various pools of water of which steam is rising. The hot water streaming in is clear and there is substancially less moss in this room then others. These pools are clearly some sort of natural hot spring. Various crystals in the walls shine colorful light inside the whole cave.
```

# Apothecary/Nursery
```
This room is split in 2 by a set of large broken doors. The shelves in this southern are filled with glassware of all sizes indicating this was some kind of apothecary. Most of the labels on the bottles have largely faded. The eastern part is filled with beds that have old and worn privacy curtains and was likely being used as a nursery. 
```
## Features
- **DC15 Investigation** or **DC12 Nature** to identify a potion.
	1. Potion of Animal Friendship
	2. Potion of Poison Resistance
	3. Antitoxin
	4. Potion of Heroism
	5. Potion of Greater Healing
	6. Potion of Hill Giant Strength
## Development
- Shadows stand a the far end of the nursery part of the room. They are hidden until a character is close enough to attack or they enter bright light.

- 2 [[Shadow]]

# Workspace
```
You enter a room containing a large workbench that is sprawling with blueprints of various items, chambers and contraptions. On the other side stands a weapon rack filled with old, rusted weapons. 
```
## Features
- Workbench & weapon rack.
- If the party has found the [[#Vault Entrance]] but not yet opened it they can find a blueprint of the altar here.
- The blueprint describes the mechanism, a **DC10 Investigation** reveals small text on the bottom that reads as followed: `Follow the first.`
- The mephits are not hiding and can be surprised.
## Enemies
- 3 [[Ancient Mud Mephit]]
	- They each start by using their summon ability.
# Shrine Room
```
The hallway leads in to a room thats mostly part of a natural cavern, in the middle stands a small shrine depicting the forest father Silvanus with a dry basin at its feet. Various glowing mushrooms light up the room with an assortment of colors.
```
## Features
- A small shrine depicting the forest father Silvanus. If the basin is filled with water the party gains **2d4 Temporary Hit Points** 
- **DC15 Investigation** reveals a faded inscription that reads: `As the forest gives, so shall it receive. As the water flows, so shall life endure.`
## Development
- **DC13 Perception** to prevent being surprised.
## Enemies
- [[Minor Earth Elemental]]
- 2 [[Needle Blight]]
# Store Room
```
Barrels, Boxes, shelves and various other containers on either sides of the room are covered by thick webs and dust. A smell of wood rot and ale hangs in the air.
```
## Features
- A barrel filled with Ale, if drunk from 2d10 Poison damage on failed **DC15 Constitution** save. **DC12 Nature** check to detect the poison.
- Webs are **DC10 Dex or Strength** to not be restrained. Repeat with action to break free.
- Webs have 10AC, 10HP.
## Development
- **DC13 Perception** to spot the spiders in advance, otherwise surprised.
## Enemies
- 3 [[Giant Spider]]
## Treasure
Spiders can be looted for.
- 1 [[Simple Toxin]]
The room contains.
- 50ft. of rope.
- A bottle of quality wine.
# Long Hallway
```
As you are walking down this 100ft hallway you hear a loud bang behind you. As you turn around you see a large boulder roll towards you. 
```
## Development
- **DC25 Athletics** to stop the boulder.
- Deals 2d10 Bludgeoning damage on a failed **DC15 Dexterity** save.
- If the boulder reaches the end of the hallways and there are still players in it, it turns around and comes back for them.
## Treasure
A crushes skeleton lies on the ground.
- A pouch containing 87gp. Its stuck requiring **DC10 Athletics** to free (Uses Action)
# Vault Entrance
```
You enter a room with a large stone door on the opposing side. The door has no visible handles or keyholes but before it stands a round altar with 5 gemstones inlaid in it. An Emerald, a Ruby, an Opal, a Pearl and a Nephrite. The room is lit up by a glowing crystal attached to a chandelier.
```
## Development
- Touching the gemstones in the following order opens the door: 
	1. **O**pal
	2. **P**earl
	3. **E**merald
	4. **N**ephrite
- The Ruby is a red herring.
# Vault
```
As you input the correct sequence the door slowly rises, revealing a large natural cavern behind it. The cavern is overgrown with plants and vines and in the middle stands the stump of a tree. A large green gemstone hovers above it, radiating with power. The emerald resembles the shape of a human heart, although several times larger, and is framed by interwoven wooden branches. 
```
If touched by a character.
```
The artifact lights up as you touch it, shining a brief blinding light. When you open your eyes it has transformed into a much smaller version of itself, now with a cord. This pendant can be worn around your neck. But before you can do so the tree stump the artifact was hovering over starts to shake and reveals itself as a monsterous mass of of tangled roots, moss and other vegetation. Slowly the creature frees itself from the ground with the sound of branches breaking, earth turning and water dripping. It lifts its large arms as it prepares to attack and lets out a deep roar.
```
