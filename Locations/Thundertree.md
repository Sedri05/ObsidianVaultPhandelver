# General Features
```
Gradually, the trail becomes an old, overgrown lane winding between dilapidated building choked with vines and brush. In the middle of the settlement rises a steep hill, upon which stands a stone tower with a partially collapsed roof and an adjoining cottage. A dirt road hugs the base of the hill and wends its way between old stone houses, many of which are roofless ruins. Other buildings appear intact. The whole place is eerily silent. A wooden sign is nailed to a post nearby. It reads: "Danger! Plant monsters and zombies! Turn back now!"
```
## Buildings
Many buildings crumbled, nature threatens to swallow the remains. 
### Ruined buildings
- 5-8ft high walls. 
- No roofs. 
- Difficult terrain inside.
### Intact Buildings
-  Wooden doors are stuck, **DC10 Athletics** check to open.
- Windows covered by wooden shutters, gives 3/4ths cover.
- Dusty furnishings inside.
## Trees and Brush
- 30-40ft tall.
- Brush counts as difficult terrain.

# U7 Old Tower
```
At the top of the hill stands a round tower with a cottage attached. Both are in relatively good condition, although half of the tower's roof is gone. A door leads into the cottage, and several arrow-slit windows are visible in the tower. You notice an eerie quiet that is occasionally broken by a loud gust of wind. The inside of the tower reeks of decomposition.
```
## Cottage
- Cottage is filled with boxes of junk
## 1st Floor
```
As you climb up the ladder you enter the 1st floor of the tower, on the ground in front of you is a grid with magic circles with seemingly random colors. Behind you hang various paintings of dragons.
```
### Development
If the characters investigate the paintings they notice one of the paintings in of a 5 headed dragon. A **DC10 History** reveals this dragon is Tiamat. Regardless of the success of the check the heads are in the following order: `White, Blue, Red, Green, Black`. 
The circles on the ground are Glyphs of Warding, if a character steps on a Glyph that is not in the order listed above they make a **DC13 Dexterity save**, on a failure take `dice: 5d8` Acid (Green), Fire (Red), Cold (White), Lightning (Blue) or Thunder (Black) Damage, half as much if they succeeded.
## Second Floor
```
Arriving on the 2nd floor you see the green lord in front of you. A green dragon lies asleep on the floor in front of you. Various offerings and corpses of giant spiders lie in front of it, in the middle of the mess you recognize the staff the druid seeks.
```
### Development
Bases on how many Glyphs were set off before the difficulty of stealing an item from its hoard increases.
It starts as a **DC5 Stealth**:
- +5 per Glyph set off.
- +1 Per successive item stolen.
- Advantage if invisible.
If at any point the dragon is woken up he is willing to bargain. He gives them the [[Venomfang#Obtain the mysterious item]] quest, in return he lets the party leave alive.

```

```

- Interior is dusty with a small stove, a table, two chairs, and a bunkbed.
## Development
Door is barred from the inside, **DC20 Athletics** to open.
Shutters are also barred from the inside, **DC15 Athletics** to open.
If they knock on the door the Cultists open.
If the characters enter the house the Cultists ask what the characters are doing. They prefer not to fight. 
A man named [[Favric]] is trying to gain favor of the Green Lord. The Cultists don't EVER reveal the Green Lord is a green dragon.
If the characters gain favor or capture a cultists they can learn the staff has been brought to [[#U7 Old Tower]], where the Green Lord resides.
