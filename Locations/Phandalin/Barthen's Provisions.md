The biggest trading post in [[Phandalin]].
Owned by [[Elmina Barthen]].
Open from sunup till sundown.
Does not sell armor or weapons, if asked the characters get directed to [[Lionshield Coster]].
### Goods
Ordinary goods, e.g. backpacks, bedrolls, rope, rations, etc.
Adventuring gear worth less than 25gp.

# Quests
- [x] #Quest 
If the initial goods are delivered here, Elmina pays the party 10gp for each member.

## Shop Notes
- **Hours:** Typically open during the day; adjust to the party's schedule.
- **Payment:** Prices are listed in gp, sp, or cp.
- **Availability:** `In stock` means available now; `Limited` means the listed quantity is all that is on the shelf; `Order` means it can be sourced with time, subject to DM discretion.
- **Negotiation:** Barthen may offer a small discount to regular customers or adventurers who bring useful news or trade goods.
- **Restocking:** Ordinary goods can generally be replenished in 1d4 days if Phandalin's supply lines are functioning. Unusual items may take longer or be unavailable.

## Selling Goods to Barthen
Barthen buys useful, resalable goods when he has the coin and storage space. A simple rule is to offer **about half the listed price** for ordinary undamaged equipment, less for damaged, bulky, stolen, or difficult-to-resell goods. This is a DM guideline, not a fixed rule.

| Goods offered | Suggested offer | Conditions |
|---|---:|---|
| Undamaged standard gear | ½ listed price | Only if he can resell it |
| Food or perishables | ¼–½ listed price | Depends on freshness and demand |
| Bulk trade goods | Negotiated | Depends on storage and buyers |
| Damaged equipment | 0–¼ listed price | May be useful only for salvage |
| Unusual or suspicious goods | No fixed offer | Barthen may decline |
## Restock and Availability Tracker
Update quantities as the party shops. Suggested quick procedure:
1. Subtract each purchased quantity from the `Stock` column.
2. Ordinary supplies restock in `1d4` days when trade routes are safe.
3. Limited items restock in `1d6 + 2` days, if available from a supplier.
4. Order-only items require DM approval and a reasonable delivery time.
5. If Phandalin is threatened or isolated, pause or reduce restocking.
## Quick Shop Interaction
**Barthen's manner:** Practical, courteous, and focused on keeping the business running. He knows the town's ordinary needs and may ask what brings the party to Phandalin, but he does not automatically know secret information.
**Useful roleplay prompts:**
- “If it's ordinary gear you need, I can likely help. Anything special will take time.”
- “Supplies have been moving steadily. Tell me what you need and I'll check the shelves.”
- “I can pay for goods I can sell, but I can't tie up all my coin in curiosities.”
