Trading post owned by the guild master, [[Halia Thornton]] in [[Phandalin]].
Local miners have their valuable finds weighed, measured, and paid out here.
In absence of any local authority, the exchange also serves as an unofficial records office, registering claims to various streams and excavations around the area.
The exchange is a great place to meet people who spend a lot of time in the countryside surrounding Phandalin.
