An inn in [[Phandalin]] ran by [[Toblen Stonehill]]. 
Has 5 free rooms, the 6th being taken up by [[Sildar Hallwinter]].

# Chapter Two
When the players arrive in town [[Sildar Hallwinter]] will suggest visiting this inn for lodgings.
When the players are looking for an Inn, read the following passage.
```
In the center of town, opposite the town green, stands a large, newly built roadhouse of fieldstone and rough-hewn timbers. The common room is filled with locals nursing mugs of ale or cider, all of them eyeing you with curiosity.
```
