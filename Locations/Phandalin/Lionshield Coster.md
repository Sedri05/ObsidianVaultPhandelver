## Shop Notes
- **Specialty:** Weapons, armor, shields, and related adventuring gear.
- **Availability:** `In stock` items are available now; `Limited` items have only the listed quantity; `Order` items require time and DM approval.
- **Pricing:** Prices generally follow the standard D&D 5e equipment lists. Adjust them to match your campaign's economy.
- **Restocking:** Common gear may arrive in `1d4 + 1` days when trade routes are operating. Specialized or expensive equipment may take longer.
- **Local situation:** The shop's supply and security can change in response to events in Phandalin and along nearby roads.

## Buying Equipment from Adventurers
A simple DM guideline for purchases from the party:

| Item condition                         |       Suggested offer | Notes                            |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------: | -------------------------------- |
| Undamaged, standard weapon or shield   |        ½ listed price | Only if the shop can resell it   |
| Undamaged, standard armor              |        ½ listed price | May require time to find a buyer |
| Damaged equipment                      |      0–¼ listed price | Depends on repair cost           |
| Unusual, oversized, or unfamiliar gear |            Negotiated | Linene may decline               |
| Clearly stolen goods                   | Refuse or investigate | Use campaign context             |

## Restock Tracker
1. Subtract sold quantities from the `Stock` column.
2. Common weapons, ammunition, and shields may restock in `1d4 + 1` days if deliveries are available.
3. Armor and less common martial weapons may take `1d6 + 2` days.
4. Expensive armor and specialist weapons are order-only unless you decide otherwise.
5. If roads are unsafe or Phandalin is isolated, reduce stock and delay deliveries.

## Quick Shop Interaction
**Linene Graywind:** A capable, businesslike merchant who takes the security of her stock seriously. She can discuss practical equipment and orders, but need not know every rumor or secret in the region.
**Roleplay prompts:**
- “Tell me what you need. I can sell you what's on the rack, but special orders take time.”
- “Keep your weapons in good condition. A dull edge won't help you when trouble finds you.”
- “I buy what I can resell. Bring me sound equipment, and we'll discuss a fair price.”
