# General Features
- Well build chambers with flagstone floors and walls of dressed stone blocks.
- West end is lower then the east end.
- 10ft high ceilings
- Doors made of wood with iron handles, hinges and build in locks.

# R1 Cellar
### Important
- Too much noise -> Bandits come from R2
- [[#Cistern]] -> Waterproof satchel
- 
```
The door opens on to a 5-foot wide landing that is 15 feet above the large cellar, with stone steps descending to the floor in two short flights. Another door stands beneath the stairs to the north. A large stone cistern occupies the western part of the room, whose walls are lined with kegs and barrels.
```
The Redbrands want to keep their base of operations hidden, so other than the barrels filled with supplies, nothing in this cellar gives away their presence. 
## Features
### Barrels
Filled with salted port, beef, flour, sugar, apples and ale. Moving barrels makes noise.
### Cistern
Rectangular reservoir, clean, filled with cold, fresh water, 10ft deep, rim 2ft higher then the floor, drainpipes from the roof fill it.
Waterproof satchel hangs from a submerged rope. Satchel can't be seen from above the water. [[Iarno Glasstaff Albrek|Glasstaff]] emergency kit.
## Development
Bandits will notice if the characters make too much noise. Will surprise the characters if unseen.
Last bandit might reveal the secret door.

# R3 Trapped Hall
## Important
- Hidden [[#Pitfall Trap]] 
```
Thick dust covers the flagstones of this somber hallway. The walls are decorated with faux columns every 10ft, and the double door at the west end of the hall is sheathed in copper plate, now green with age. A relief carving a mournfim angel adorns the door.
```
## Features
### Pitfall Trap
Pit trap in the middle of the hallway. Hidden under false flooring consisting of loose stone tiles laid atop breakaway timbers. Collapses under >100 pounds (1 person). **DC 15 Perception** check to discover. Passable on the sides with **DC 10 Acrobatics**.
#### Falling in the trap
20ft deep.
If fallen in make **DC 15 Dex** save to catch the edge. On Fail take 2d6 bludgeoning damage + prone.

# R8 Crevasse Cave
## Important
- Tunnel to the outside. 
- [[Ssarnak]] guards this cave.
- South Bridge is rigged.
- Crevasse with various corpses & treasure.
```
A cold breeze softly sighs in this large natural cavern, carrying the scent of decaying flesh. A crevasse with sloped walls divides the cavern and is flanked by 2 rough stone columns that support the 20ft high ceiling. Two arched wooden bridges span the chasm.
```
## Features
### Bridges
Wooden Planks, No railings. Can be dislodged by anyone.
South Bridge is rigged if >50lbs stands on it. Trap can be detected with **DC10 Perception** check.
### Tunnel
Rough hewn passage south of the room, continues for 100 feet in to a cave south of the [[Tresendar Manor]], excellent way to smuggle goods in to [[Phandalin]]. [[Carp Alderleaf]] knows of this tunnel.
### Crevasse 
5-10ft wide, 20ft deep, walls are easily climbable. Falling in you take **2d6 bludgeoning** damage.
Feels unusually cold, **Detect Magic** reveals faint necromantic aura, matter in here decomposes at half the normal rate.
- Skeleton of [[Thel Dendrar]] can be found at the bottom with a pendant, [[Mirna Dendrar]] can identify him or the pendant.
- Underneath the south bridge are corpses of [[Psi Goblins]]. Clearly different from regular goblins. Killed by big claw marks because they were wreaking havoc in the hideout. 
### Treasure
[[Ssarnak]] keeps its hoard in a battered wooden chest under the north bridge. Can't be seen from above, instantly seen when inside the crevasse.
- 136gp
- 5 Malachite Gems | 12gp each
- 2 Healing Potions
- Potion Of Climbing
- Potion Of Growth
- [[Gambler's Warhammer]]

# R11 Wizard's Workshop
## Important
- Can listen to door.
- Brewing invisibility potion.
- Tomb written in dwarvish.
```
This rooms appears to be a wizard's workshop. A large worktable is set up with alembics, retorts, distillation coils, and other alchemical devices, all of them stewing and bubbling away. Bookshelves are crowded with sheaves of parchment and strange-looking tomes.
```
## Information
### Listening to the door
Before a character opens the door make a **DC15 Perception** check, on success they hear faint bubbling & dripping sounds.
### The familiar
[[Zeond]] watches over his masters work, does not immediately attack, only doing so when the party is already in battle.
### Books and Notes
Scattered books & notes on alchemical mixtures. Proficiency in arcana lets a character discover he was brewing potions of invisibility, unsuccessfully. 
There is also a tome written in dwarvish, describing the history of Phandelver and the [[Forge of Spells]].  READ CHAPTER 1
The book records that a magic mace named Lightbringer was commissioned by priests of Lathander, the god of dawn, from mages working with the gnomes and dwarves of the Phandelver Pact. The mace was lost when [[Wave Echo Cave]] and its mine vanished from history.
## Development
### Telepathic bond
[[Zeond]] shares a telepathic bond with [[Iarno Glasstaff Albrek]], because of this Glasstaff has time to prepare for an attack.
