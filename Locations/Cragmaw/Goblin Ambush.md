4 goblins ambush the characters on the road from [[Neverwinter]] to [[Phandalin]]. They stand on both sides of the road.

## Development
When all but 1 goblin remain, that goblin will run away through the [[#Trail to the Cragmaw Hideout|Goblin Trail]].
The characters can knock a goblin out with unarmed or bludgeoning damage. After the goblin wakes up it can be convinced (DC 5) to reveal the following information.
#### Bugbear Leader
Their leader is a bugbear named [[Klarg]]. Klarg reports to [[King Grol]], leader of the Cragmaw's who is in [[Cragmaw Castle]]. The goblin can provide directions to the castle. It's about 20 miles north-east.
#### Capturing Gundren
[[Klarg]] received a message from [[King Grol]] that said that someone named the [[Nezzar The Spider||Spider]] was paying them to capture [[Gundren Rockseeker]] and his cargo. And to bring both to [[Cragmaw Castle]].
#### Sildar's Location
[[Gundren Rockseeker||Gundren's]] human companion is being held in the [[Cragmaw Hideout#H6 Goblin Den||"eating cave"]]. Roughly 15 goblins dwell in this hideout.
#### Strange Goblins
Recently, [[Psi Goblins|"strange goblins"]] have joined the [[Cragmaw|Cragmaws]] in the roadside ambushes. The goblins are scared of them and advice the characters to be too.

## Trail to the Cragmaw Hideout
By passing a **DC 10 Survival** check the player can discover a trail to the [[Cragmaw Hideout]].
Alternatively a goblin can be convinced to lead the way and show the trail, this also lets the characters avoid all traps on the way.
If not found, the characters continue to [[Phandalin]]
#### Snare
After 10 minutes of travelling the character make a **DC 15 Perception** check or the front character falls for a snare trap. If that character fails a **DC 10 Dex** save they get caught and dangle 10ft above the ground. A character who isn't lowered carefully takes **1d6 bludgeoning** damage.

#### Pit Trap
After another 10 minutes the characters must make a **DC 15 Perception** check or the front character falls for a pit trap. That character must make a **DC 10 Dex** save or fall 10ft and take **1d6 bludgeoning** damage.

After all of that the characters reach the [[Cragmaw Hideout]].
