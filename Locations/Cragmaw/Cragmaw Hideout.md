### H1 Cave Mouth
When the characters arrive read the following passage
```
The goblin trail ends at the cave in a hillside 5 miles from the scene of the ambush. A shallow stream flows from the cave moutj, which is screened by dense briars thickets. A narrow, dry path leads into the cave on the right side of the stream.
```
#### Features
Stream is 2 feet deep, cold, slow, not difficult to cross.
The thickets is impenetrable from the west side and provide 3/4th cover.
#### Development
The goblins from H2 can ambush the characters if they take too long or make too much noise.
+ Party gets **surprised**
+ [[#Enemies]]
### H2 Goblin Guard Post
When characters cross to the east side of the stream read the following passage.
```
On the east side of the stream that flows from the cave mouth, a small area in the briar thickets has been hollowed out to form a lookout outpost. Wooden planks flatten the briars and provide room for guards to lie hidden and watch the area, including several goblins lurking there right now
```
#### Features
The thicket provides **3/4ths cover**.
#### Development
If the characters are not sneaking, they enter battle with the goblins.
- **DC 9 Sneak** check to pass the goblins.
If the Goblin Boss dies, the rest flee to area [[#H3 Kennel]].

**Once this combat is over, the characters level up.**
#### Enemies
2 [[Goblin|Goblins]]
1 [[Goblin Boss]] | 15sp


### H3 Kennel
When the characters can see inside the first opening on the right, read the following passage.
```
Just inside the cave mouth, a few uneven stone steps ascend to a small, dank chamber on the east side of the passage. The cave narrows to a steep fissure at the far end and is filled with the stench of animals. Three chained wolves snarl and rattle their chains as you approach the cave mouth. Each wolf's chain leads to a single rod.
```
#### Fissure
Narrow opening at the end of the room that leads to a 30ft climb.
Requires **DC 10 Athletics** check
- **10+:** Move at half speed.
- **6-9:** Takes character 10 mins to climb.
- **>=5:** 3d6 fall damage.
#### Development
If the wolves see a player they make a **DC 15 Strength** check to loosen the chain. 
After this they make a **DC 10 Strength** check to get free and attack.
Goblins in the area can use their action to set the wolves free.
#### Calming the wolves
The wolves can be calmed with a **DC 15 Animal Handling** check or a **DC 10** if given food.
#### Enemies
3 [[Wolf|Wolves]]
(After 2 rounds of combat) 1d4 [[Goblin|Goblins]]

### H4 Steep passage
If the characters go past H3 read the following passage.
```
The main passage from the cave mouth climbs steeply upward, the stream plunging and splashing down its west side. In the shadows, a side passage leads west across the other side of the stream.
```
If the characters are using darkvision or a light source read the following passage.
```
In the shadows of the ceiling to the north, you can just make out the dim shape of a rope bridge crossing over the passage ahead of you. Another passage 20 feet above the floor appears to intersect this one.
```
#### Development
Any character that succeeds a **DC 15 Perception** check can see a goblin guarding on **top of the bridge**.
The goblin notices the characters if they have a light source or are not sneaking (**DC 12 Stealth** check).
If not stopped the goblin will warn its allies and trigger the [[#Flood]], after which it attacks with it's bow.
#### Western passage
Difficult terrain.
There is a giant poisonous snake in the passage that attacks any creature that disturbs it.
- Surprised unless **Passive Perception** is **13+** or not running from flood.
- If still there, goblin on the bridge notices the fight and triggers the [[#Flood]]
Any creature moving or making a melee attack must make a **DC 10 Acrobatics** check or trigger a collapse. Nearby creatures must make a **DC 10 Dex save** or take **2d6 bludgeoning damage** and fall **prone**. When you succeed take half as much damage.
#### Enemies
1 [[Goblin]]
1 [[Giant Poisonous Snake]] | Has **advantage** while attacking from slope

### H5 Overpass
If passing by the western passage read the following passage.
```
The stream continues beyond another set of uneven steps ahead, bending eastward. A waterfall burbles in a larger cavern somewhere ahead of you.
```

#### Rope Bridge
The bridge is 20ft above the stream and has 10 hit points and 5 AC with vulnerability to fire and slashing damage.
A character can climb the rock wall to reach the bridge with a **DC 15 Athletics** check.
If the bridge collapsed any character on it must make a **DC 10 Dex** save to dart across the bridge or fall and take **2d6 bludgeoning** damage.

#### Flood
If the goblins are warned they can break the walls of the pools in H7 to release a powerful stream of water down the main passage of the hideout. Then read the following passage.
```
The passage fills with a mighty roar as a huge surge of rushing water pours from above.
```
Any creature on the low ground of the main passage must make a **DC 10 Dex** save. A creature that fails this save must make a **DC 15 Strength** save or is swept away to H1 and takes **2d6 bludgeoning** damage and is knocked prone.
A second wave can be released if the goblin on the bridge sees a player character and tells his allies.

### H6 Goblin Den
When the characters get a view of this area read the following passage.
```
This large cave is divided in half by a ten-foot-high escarpment. A steep, natural staircase leads from the lower portion to the upper ledge. The air is hazy with the smoke of a cooking fire, and pungent from the smell of poorly cured hides and unwashed goblin.
```
Characters can avoid being spotted if they attempt to approach quietly and make a **DC 14 Stealth** check. If failed or not hiding, the goblins will attack the characters.
#### Development
If the goblins are losing, [[Yeemik]] will grab [[Sildar Hallwinter]] and drag him to the upper ledge and say `Truce, or this human dies!`. [[Errk]] shouts supportively. He attempts to make a deal with the characters; Kill the head of the Cragmaw Hideout [[Klarg]] and bring him his head in return for Sildar.
Sildar groggily warns the characters that they shouldn't trust the goblins.
- If accepted and brought the head, Yeemik will attempt to extort the characters for Sildar for the price of 50gp.
- If refused or attacked further at any point he will push Sildar off the ledge. Sildar will drop to 0 hit points and roll death saving throws. Can be stabilized with a **DC 10 Medicine** check, healer's kit or other forms of healing.
#### Saving Sildar
Once [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] is pushed off the ledge he will drop to 0 hit points and roll death saving throws. He can be stabilized with a **DC 10 Medicine** check, healer's kit or other forms of healing.

After the battle Sildar can provide the following information: [[Sildar Hallwinter#Information|Sildar Dialog]]
Sildar tells the characters that he intends to continue to Phandalin and will pay the party 50gp if they escort him. Currently he has no money but will pay the characters a day after arriving in [[Phandalin]]. He will join the party and fight with a shortsword picked up from the goblins.

### H7 Twin Pool Cave
If the characters enter this room read the following passage. Alter it if the pools have been drained.
```
This cavern is half-filled with two large pools of water. A narrow waterfall high in the eastern wall feeds the pool, which drains out the western end of the chamber to form the stream that flows from the cave mouth below. Low fieldstone walls serve as dams holding the water in. A wide exit stands to the south, while two smaller passages lead west. The sound of the waterfall echoes through the cavern, making it difficult to hear.
```

When the characters enter this room, a fight instantly breaks out. One of the goblins will attempt to warn [[Klarg]] in [[#H8 Klarg's Cave]]. 
#### Enemies
3 [[Goblin|Goblins]]
2 [[Wolf|Wolves]]

### H8 Klarg's Cave
When the players enter this room, read the following passage.
```
Sacks and crates of looted provisions are piled in the south end of this large cave. To the west, the floor slopes toward a narrow opening that descends into darkness. A larger opening leads north down a set of natural stone steps, and the roar of falling water echoes from beyond. In the middle of the cavern, the coals of a large fire smolder. At the far end of the cave you see Gundren unconcious on the ground. Next to him stands a dark hooded figure whose cloak is fastened with the holy symbol of the spider queen, Lolth.
```

If [[Klarg]] is warned, he hides and attempts to surprise the characters. Any Character with a **Passive Perception of 14** does not get surprised. 
Otherwise the characters can surprise Klarg if they enter the area from [[#Fissure]].
The hooded figure ([[Nezzar The Spider]]) will use dimension door as soon as combat starts, bypassing the initiative order. While leaving he tells Klarg to `Clear up this mess, would you Klarg?`. He takes [[Gundren Rockseeker]] with him.
#### Development
If Klarg is the last one alive he will attempt to flee through the natural chimney. To do so he must pass a **DC 10 Dex** save. If he fails he becomes restrained. To free himself he must spend an action and pass a **DC 10 Dex** save.
#### Fire Pit
A firepit sits at the centre of the room. Any creature in it takes **1d4 fire** damage. It emits dim light.
#### Natural Chimney
A natural chimney sits at the west side of the room. It descends 30ft. See [[#Fissure]]
#### Supplies
Piles of sacks and crates provide half cover to any creature.
They are marked with a symbol of a blue lion. If the characters pass a **DC 10 History** check they know this symbol is of the [[Lionshield Coster]], a merchant company, they hold a trading post in [[Phandalin]].
A treasure chest sits among the supplies. It holds.
- 1700cp
- 150sp
- 2 Healing potions
- Jade statuette of a frog with tiny golden orbs for eyes | 40gp
