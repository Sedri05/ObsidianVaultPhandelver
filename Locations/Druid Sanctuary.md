# Store Room
```
Barrels, Boxes, shelves and various other containers on all sides of the room are covered by thick webs and dust. A smell of wood rot and ale hangs in the air.
```
## Features
- A barrel filled with Ale, if drunk from 2d10 Poison damage on failed **DC15 Constitution** save. **DC12 Nature** check to detect the poison.

# Long Hallway
```
As you are walking down this 100ft hallway you hear a loud bang behind you. As you turn around you see a large boulder roll towards you. 
```
## Development
- **DC25 Athletics** to stop the boulder.
- Deals 2d10 Bludgeoning damage on a failed **DC15 Dexterity** save.
- If the boulder reaches the end of the hallways and there are still players in it, it turns around and comes back for them.
## Treasure
A crushes skeleton lies on the ground.
- A pouch containing 87gp. Its stuck requiring **DC10 Athletics** to free (Uses Action)

# Vault Entrance
```
You enter a room with a large stone door on the opposing side. The door has no visible handles or keyholes but before it stands a round altar with 5 gemstones inlaid in it. An Emerald, a Ruby, an Opal, a Pearl and a Nephrite. The room is lit up by a glowing crystal attached to a chandelier.
```
## Development
- Touching the gemstones in the following order opens the door: 
	1. **O**pal
	2. **P**earl
	3. **E**merald
	4. **N**ephrite
- The Ruby is a red herring.
