Old Owl Well is and old ruined watchtower, along with adjoining building that consist of little more then a few crumbling walls.
**Location:** 18 miles southeast of [[Conyberry]], int he hills south of the [[Triboar Trail]]. 
```
As you crest a low ridge you spy the crumbling ruins of an old watchtower and mounds of rubble enclosing a courtyard amid the rugged hills. A colorful tent sits in the middle of the courtyard, but no one is in sight.
```
12 [[Zombie]]s inside the old tower that can't be seen from outside. **DC15 Perception** check to smell their deadly odor from farther away. They attack as soon as the characters approach the tower or tent.
If battle breaks out [[Hamun Kost]] emerges from his tent and asks: `What is the meaning of this?`.
# Information
Hamun is willing to trade information. He knows the location of [[Cragmaw Castle]] and [[Wave Echo Cave]]. 
For this information he shares 1 or both requests.
## Marauders
He wants the marauders at [[Wyvern Tor]] to leave. They scouted his camp and might attack.
## Banshee
He wants to ask Agatha the banshee the name of the wizard who build the tower at Old Owl Well. Hamun is scared of the banshee which is why he wont go himself.
