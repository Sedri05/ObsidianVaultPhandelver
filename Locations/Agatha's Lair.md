The [[Triboar Trail]] runs through an the abandoned town [[Conyberry]]. It provides an easy landmark to locating the lair of the banshee [[Agatha]].

```
The forest grows dark and still as the trail winds deeper into the trees. Heavy vines and thick layers of moss drape the branches, and the air is noticably colder than it was in the ruined village. After rounding a bend in the trail, you come upon a old wooden shelter made from the warped, bowing branches of nearby trees. A low doorway leads inside.
```
When the characters enter the shelter read.
```
The shelter is split in to 2 rooms, its sparrsely furnished with shelves, a table and some chairs. On the east side stands a fireplace that hasn't been lit in years.
```
After a small amount of time Agatha confronts the characters.
```
The air grows cold, and a powerful feeling of dread grips you. A pale light flickers in the air, rapidly taking the form of a female elf, her hair and robes waving in the spectral wind. A hateful expression twists her features. She snarls: "Foolish mortals. What do you want here? Do you not know it is death to seek me out?"
```
If the characters are rude, disrespectful, or threatening, Agatha uses Horrifying Visage and subsequently attacks if the party does not back down.
# Dealing with the Banshee
If the characters are respectful they can persuade her with a **DC15 Persuasion** check or by handing her the comb. Otherwise she attacks.
```
She smiles with cold amusement and says: "Very well. I know you seek many things. Ask me one question, and i will give you an answer."
```
# Information
She can answer almost any single question correctly.
## Spellbook
She traded the spellbook to a necromancer named [[Tsernoth]] from the city of Iriaebor more than 100 years ago. She doesn't know what happened to the book after that.
## Wave Echo Cave
She can direct the characters to [[Wave Echo Cave]].
## Cragmaw Castle
She can direct the characters to [[Cragmaw Castle]].
## The Spider's Identity
She knows who [[Nezzar The Spider|The Spider]] is.
