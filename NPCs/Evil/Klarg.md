[[Bugbear]]
Leader of the [[Cragmaw Hideout]].
Has Orders from [[King Grol]] to plunder any poorly defended caravans.
Recieved a message from [[Cragmaw Castle]] to intercept and kidnap [[Gundren Rockseeker]]
The [[Cragmaw]] [[Goblin|Goblins]] generally do not like Klarg.
### Personality
Klarg refers to himself in 3rd person.
- `Who dares defy Klarg!`
- `Klarg will build a throne from your bones!`
Klarg prefers to keep the loot from ambushing and raiding in his den.
