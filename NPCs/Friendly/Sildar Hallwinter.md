# Information
Met [[Gundren Rockseeker]] in Neverwinter and agreed to accompany him to [[Phandalin]].

If saved by the characters, Sildar has the following information.
#### A missing wizard.
[[Iarno Glasstaff Albrek]], a human wizard travelled to [[Phandalin]] several months ago to try and establish order there. After the [[Lords' Alliance]] didn't hear from Iarno, Sildar decided to investigate.
#### Wave Echo Cave
The three [[Rockseeker Brothers]] recent located an entrance to the long-lost [[Wave Echo Cave]], a site of mines in the [[Phandelver Pact]] 
#### The Mysterious Spider
[[Klarg]], the bugbear who leads this goblin band, has orders to waylay [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]. Sildar heard from the goblins that "the [[Nezzar The Spider|Spider]]" sent word that the dwarf was to be brought to him. Sildar doesn't know who or what the Spider is.
#### Finding Cragmaw Castle
[[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] has a map showing the secret location of the [[Wave Echo Cave]], but the goblins took it when they captured him. Sildar believes [[Klarg]] send the map and the dwarf to the leader of the [[Cragmaw|Cragmaws]] at a place called [[Cragmaw Castle]]. Sildar suggests someone in [[Phandalin]] might know.
#### Strange goblin
A [[Psi Goblins||"strange goblin"]] was part of the gang that ambushed Sildar and [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]. Sildar has a bad feeling about this goblin. The normal goblins are scared of this strange goblin.
# Goals
### Investigation
Investigate the disappearance of his contact, [[Iarno Glasstaff Albrek]], in Phandalin.
### A Prosperous Phandalin
He wants to see Phandalin thrive, therefor he wants to find the lost mine of [[Wave Echo Cave]] and help the [[Rockseeker Brothers]] put it back in production.
