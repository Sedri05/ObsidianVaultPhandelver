# Information
### Daran's Suspicions
Daran is concerned about the [[Redbrands]] and would like to see someone teach them a lesson. He tells the party that it's time someone took a stand against their leader, [[Iarno Glasstaff Albrek|Glasstaff]]. He knows the Redbrands hang around the [[Sleeping Giant]]. He can also tell the party that the Redbrands' safe house lies under [[Tresendar Manor]], the ruin at the east edge of town.
# Goals
Resolve [[#Daran's Suspicions]] and [[#Old Owl Trouble]]. If the party does so he will approach them and urges them to join the [[Order of the Gauntlet#Joining The Order|Order of the Gauntlet]].

# Quests
### Old Owl Trouble
- [x] #Quest
Daran has heard stories from prospectors in the hills northeast of [[Phandalin]] that someone is digging in the ruins know as [[Old Owl Well]]. More disturbingly, several prospectors have reported being chased from the area by foul undead. He asks the characters to head to the ruins, a couple of days march northeast of Phandalin, and find out who's there and what they're doing. Daran knows the ruins are an old watchtower of ancient magical empire known as [[Netheril]], and he worries that dangerous magic might be dormant there. Daran is willing to give them his [[Boots of Striding and Springing]]. These magical boots came in handy when Daran adventured, but he has no use for them anymore.
