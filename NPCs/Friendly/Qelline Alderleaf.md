# Information
### Qelline's Friend
Qelline is a longtime friend of a druid named [[Reidoth]]. If Qelline realizes the characters are looking for specific sites in the area, such as the [[Cragmaw Castle]], she suggests the characters visit Reidoth and ask her for help, `since there's not an inch of the land the druid doesn't know`. Qelline tells the party Reidoth recently set out for the ruins of a town called [[Thundertree]], just west of the [[Neverwinter Wood]]. The ruins are about 50 miles northwest of [[Phandalin]], and Qelline provides directions so the party can easily find the place.
