# Information
### Carp's Story
He was playing in the woods near [[Tresendar Manor]] when he found a secret [[Redbrand Hideout#Tunnel|tunnel]] in the thicket. A couple of "Big scary bandits" came out of the tunnel and met with the [[Redbrands]]. They didn't see him, but it was close. Carp thinks the bandits have a secret lair under the old manor house. He also saw some strange goblins with "glowing hands" duck into the tunnel. Carp can lead the party to the tunnel or just give them the directions. The tunnel leads to the [[Redbrand Hideout]].
