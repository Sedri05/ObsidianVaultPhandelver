# Information
### Halia's Job Offer
- [x] #Quest 
If the party asks about the [[Redbrands]], she can describe how the ruffians loiter around the [[Sleeping Giant]] tap house and have a base under [[Tresendar Manor]], on the east edge of town. She offers the party 100gp to capture the Redbrands' leader, whom the outlaws call [[Iarno Glasstaff Albrek|Glasstaff]], and bring her any correspondence found in his quarters. The party should be weary of her because she indents to take over the Redbrands for herself. A **DC 15 Insight** check reveals Halia has ulterior motives for removing the leader.
